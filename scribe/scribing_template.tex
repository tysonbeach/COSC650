\section{Scribe duties}

Scribing lectures is a part of class participation (10\% of the course).  

The scribe notes will be evaluated based on mathematical correctness, clarity of writing, faithfulness to what was covered in lecture, and inclusion of bibliographic pointers to relevant literature. 

\section{TeX conventions}

Please refrain from using a separate style file; instead define any macros that you need within this TeX file.

For the bibliography, directly add BibTeX entries to \texttt{refs.bib} and include that file as an attachment when you email your scribe notes.
